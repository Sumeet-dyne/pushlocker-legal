Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

PushLocker is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains exactly what the app does — and what it never does.

Camera and pose detection

Camera frames are processed entirely on your device to detect body position and count exercise repetitions. Frames are analysed in memory in real time and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted to any server. No photos or videos leave your device.

Data stored on your device

Your workout history, daily goals, blocked-app configuration, alarm settings, profile name and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server and do not receive this data.

Screen Time / app blocking

Focus mode uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. The apps you choose to block and your usage information are handled by iOS on your device. We do not receive or store the list of apps you select.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and alarms are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details, and we do not receive your name or billing address from Apple.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share personal information. The app does not request permission to track you.

Data retention and deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you control it entirely. Deleting the app removes your local data from that device.

Children

PushLocker is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@pushlocker.app
