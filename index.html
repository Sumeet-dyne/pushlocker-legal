How it works

Three steps to a phone that earns its place

No willpower required — the apps only open when your body pays for them.

1

Pick the apps to block

Choose any apps from Apple's Screen Time picker. PushLocker shields them with the system shield, so they stay locked even out of the app.

2

Set your reps

Decide your unlock target — a number of pushups, squats or crunches, or a timed set. Adjust it per app whenever you like.

3

Work out to unlock

Open a blocked app and PushLocker counts your reps with the camera in real time. Finish the set and the app unlocks.

Support

Questions, answered

How does the camera counting work?

Frames from your camera are processed entirely on your iPhone to detect your body position and count reps. They are analysed in memory in real time and are never recorded, saved or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Which apps can I block?

Any app you pick in Apple's Screen Time picker. You can block several apps at once and set a different rep target for each one.

Can I skip or cheat the workout?

Strict Mode locks your workout settings for the session so the target can't be lowered mid-workout. You can always close the app, but the block stays in place until the reps are done.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and cancel there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Renewal and cancellation terms are in the Terms of Use.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up, no login and no server. Your data stays on your device.

Email us and we'll get back to you. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug.
